Unit II - Securing AI Systems
-  Model, data, and pipeline security controls
- Protecting training data, model weights, and inference endpoints
- Secure AI deployment in cloud, on-prem, and hybrid infrastructures
- Threats: model theft, inversion, poisoning, evasion, membership inference
- Privacy-preserving AI methods: differential privacy, federated learning, synthetic data
-  Secure design principles for AI system architecture

Unit III - AI-Assisted Security
- AI-enabled anomaly and threat detection
- SOC workflow automation: event triage, alert correlation, response orchestration
- Behavior analytics and UEBA
- AI-driven threat modeling and continuous monitoring

Unit IV - Governance, Risk, Compliance, and Privacy
- Regulatory and governance frameworks: GDPR, EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC AI standards
- AI governance and accountability structures
- Risk management across the AI lifecycle
- Responsible AI: ethics, fairness, transparency, explainability
- Documentation and auditability: model cards, data sheets
